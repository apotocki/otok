Privacy Policy for Otok Hex - HEX File Viewer

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

The application runs locally on your iPhone or iPad. It does not collect any data, does not use analytics or advertising, and does not send any data to the developer or to third parties.

The owner of the application, Alexander Pototskiy, does not have any way to access the data viewed or created by the user.

Files on your device

You can open any file you choose with the system document picker or from the Files app. The application works only with the files you open and with the files in its own documents folder, which the Files app shows as "Otok Hex".

Network access (HTTP server)

The application contains an HTTP server that is off by default and is started only by you. While it is on, you can browse, download and upload the files of the application's documents folder from a web browser on another device in your local network, at the address shown in the application. Access can optionally be protected with a login and password, set where the server is turned on. Files are transferred only between your device and the browser connected to it and are not sent anywhere else.

The connection is not encrypted (plain HTTP), so use the server only in networks you trust.

The server stops when you turn it off or when the application goes to the background.

Camera

The application uses the camera only to scan QR codes and barcodes, and only when you start scanning. The images are processed on your device and are neither stored nor sent anywhere.

In-app purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The application receives from Apple only the status of your purchases; it never sees your payment details.

Crash reports

If you chose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may provide the developer with crash reports of the application. They contain technical information about the crash (such as the functions being called) and do not contain your files.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We will let You know via email and/or a prominent notice on Our Service, prior to the change becoming effective and update the "Last updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy.

You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.
